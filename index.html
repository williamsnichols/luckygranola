Our Story

Lucky Granola was started with a simple craving: a breakfast that felt both nourishing and indulgent. As a first-gen Asian American, I've always loved starting my mornings with my yogurt and granola ritual. But after a while, I found myself craving something different. When I looked at different granola brands, I couldn't help but wonder — why do they all offer the same flavors?

I started experimenting in my own kitchen, using Asian pantry staples to create bold new flavors like miso, sesame, yuzu, and matcha — all made with wholesome, natural ingredients. What began as a kitchen experiment quickly grew, as friends and family kept asking for more.

Whether you're enjoying it over yogurt or snacking straight out of the bag, Lucky Granola brings a fresh take, and a little luck, to the start of your day.
